# Re-review of the PM2.5 → PVT analysis (2026-09-28)

## Verdict
The headline result (pooled β = −6.4e-4 Hz per µg/m³, p = 0.040) is not justifiable. The cause
is a timezone bug, not statistics, and it invalidates every PM2.5/temp/RH number in PLAN.md. With
timing fixed and nothing else changed, the plan-of-record model gives β = −2.0e-4, p = 0.18.
That is not a demonstrated null: every pre-test spec is negative, and the CI [−4.7, +0.6] cannot
exclude ~4 ms per within-rider SD. It does exclude the original point estimate (−6.4). Post-test
placebo air is at least as predictive as pre-test air, which points to day-level / calendar
confounding rather than an acute exposure effect.

Provenance: R0–R2, S1–S2 and N1–N2 were declared in REANALYSIS_SPEC before any corrected fit.
The specification curve, the session_number/temperature diagnostics and the region split were
added after seeing R0/R1/N1, so treat them as post-hoc.

## 1. Timezone bug (build_pvt_aq_dataset.py — fixed 2026-10-01)
- `row.timestamp.timestamp()` treats naive Bangkok-local PVT times as UTC, so "test time" is 7h late.
- `dt.datetime.combine(test_date, time.min).timestamp()` uses the host tz (America/Los_Angeles), so
  "day start" = 15:00 Bangkok (14:00 after the 2026-03-08 US DST switch).
- Effective window = [15:00 BKK, test+7h]. Of 1755 sessions with exposure: 809 (46%) have windows
  entirely after the test and 394 mostly after. On average only 30% of the air in the
  "primary exposure" preceded the test. The 1h/4h windows are wholly 6–7h / 3–7h after the test.
  Examples: an 08:46 test gets window 15:00→15:46; 13:49 → 15:00→20:49; 21:33 → 15:00→00:00.
- Corroboration: shift_duration_min = 60·hour − 479.5 (pre-DST) / − 419.5 (post-DST) in ≥98.5%
  of sessions. That is the r = 0.988 "residual issue" and the VIF ≈ 2e6. Tests before 07:00 get
  0% coverage (91.3% overall vs the 98.1% §1 expected; corrected build: 98.0%).
- Harness: rebuilding with the buggy definitions reproduces data/pvt_aq_linked.csv exactly
  (1755/1755, max |diff| = 0.0).
- Bangkok-local verified from data: a stop-to-test dip in GPS movement (22.5% → 8.0% of minutes
  moving) around test times appears only under the Bangkok reading. It is flat under UTC or ±1h.
- Data facts the plan got wrong: the AQ export is 35 study days × full 24h at 1 Hz (sensors log at
  home overnight). "First reading of the day" is midnight, not shift start. Most nodes have GPS
  fixes ~all day (CLAUDE.md's "GPS mostly empty" is wrong for rider nodes 6011–6032).

Unweighted mean-group: −9.1, p = 0.19, 11/22 riders negative. I² falls from 44.8% to 8–21%.

Specification curve (outcome × SE × pooling × time control = 16 specs per window):
- Pre-test windows: 12/64 specs p<.05, all from onset→test. Plan's primary window: 0/16, p 0.09–0.25.
- Placebo post-test windows: 21/48 specs p<.05; test→midnight has median β −5.5 and 13/16 p<.05.
Air breathed after the test "predicts" performance at least as well as air breathed before it.
The original significant result was essentially a placebo window (afternoon/evening air).

Decision rule (pre-declared): R1 n.s. and N1 not null → not supported. N2b is inconclusive: its CI
includes the R1 estimate, so it passes its criterion. The verdict rests on R1 + N1, not on N2b.

Region split (POST-HOC, region_split.py), ×1e-4:

|---|---|---|
| Original buggy | +4.55, p=0.61 (4/10 neg) | −8.65, p=0.005 (8/12 neg) |

The original signal came entirely from Chiang Mai, where burning-season PM2.5 and the calendar
decline in performance rise together. The "positive subgroup" (Panatda, Tanapat, Ratanaphon,
Paiwan) is all Bangkok, where PM2.5 falls while performance falls. Region explains part of the
original heterogeneity (Q_between = 3.7, p = 0.055), not all of it (within-region I² = 42%).

## 3. Claim-by-claim status of PLAN.md "Results so far"
- §4 significant pooled effect: does not survive.
- "Cumulative > recent" (window sensitivity): invalid, since all windows were post-test. The
  corrected monotone pattern is matched by placebo windows, consistent with longer windows being
  better proxies for day-level conditions.
- I² = 44.8% "real heterogeneity" / positive-rider subgroup: mostly an artifact of the misaligned
  exposure (I² 8–21% corrected), partly a Bangkok/Chiang Mai split (see region table).
- Precision-sensitivity check: the logic was wrong regardless of the bug. A signed corr(β, SE) =
  0.66 is funnel asymmetry, not "noise scattering around a real effect" (which predicts
  corr(|β−μ|, SE), not a signed correlation). Dropping the high-SE riders, who were the positive
  ones, strengthens a negative pooled estimate by construction.
- §5 Pesaran–Smith rationale for the §4/§5 gap: rests on the heterogeneity that has now largely gone.
- Nonlinearity, learning-effect, τ² checks: moot (computed on invalid exposure).
- session_number decline: survives (exposure-independent), but after region×day FE its residual SD
  is 1.8 sessions (from 27.7). It cannot be separated from regional calendar time, so
  "fatigue/disengagement" is not supportable. Performance falls Jan→Apr in both regions while
  PM2.5 falls in Bangkok (64→28) and rises in Chiang Mai (31→108), so PM2.5 does not explain the
  decline. That does not rule out a small PM2.5 contribution on top of it.
  Sensor temperature rises in both regions (to ~36 °C); heat is a candidate, not a finding.
- Seasonal-confound check: its VIF/study_day arguments are moot, and its "separate phenomenon"
  conclusion overreaches for the reason above.

## 4. Issues independent of the bug
- PLAN.md calls 1/RT "pre-registered"; nothing was registered. Git shows the per-rider
  meta-analysis was fixed as primary (078c788, 11:38) before fitting code existed (4d47a87, 12:00).
  That is the defensible plan-of-record claim.
- Clustering was adopted after seeing it flip n.s. → significant (documented in PLAN.md); CR1 with
  9–43 clusters (median 32) and 7 parameters is anti-conservative. Wald-z at k = 22 is too.
- Outcome: mean(1000/RT) includes anticipations. 122 sessions shift by >0.05 Hz, max 3.6 Hz,
  larger than the whole claimed effect. It made little difference to the corrected result.
- Effect size: even the original claim was ~6 ms mean-RT-equivalent per within-rider SD (51 µg/m³),
  0.09 SD of speed. Corrected: ~1.9 ms, 0.03 SD.
- Narrative pattern: each non-significant check was rationalized ("expected with less data",
  "consistent with heterogeneity"). The write-ups should not be trusted as interpretation.

## 6. Post-hoc follow-ups (same session, all hypothesis-generating)
- Off-shift data (offshift.py): 53.9M roster readings on 643 rider-days. GPS-movement shifts (median
  09:36-19:30) cover 36% of readings; 54% are off-shift; 10% are on days with no movement.
- Home vs road (home_road.py): road median +10 ug/m3 vs home on the same rider-day (+6 same hour),
  road higher 72-77% of the time, 4x more minute-to-minute variability. But daily home and road
  means correlate r=0.75 within rider. Home carries overnight smoke episodes (Chiang Mai, Mar-Apr).
- Sensor temperature (temp_vs_ambient.py, METAR VTBD/VTCC + ERA5): road r=0.65 BKK / 0.78 CNX,
  about +3 C warm, slope ~1. Home +5-8 C warm, damped (device heat). The PVT models' temp
  covariate reads +5/+7 C warm (within-rider r 0.74/0.86). Use ambient temperature instead.
- Plan-of-record design (per-rider + meta-analysis): day-to-day PM2.5 deviations are shared across
  riders (pairwise r 0.78/0.85), so pooling reduces noise but not common-calendar bias. Residuals
  are not cross-correlated (CD ~ -0.6), so the problem is bias, not SE.
- Regional sign flip (region_sign.py): PM2.5 falls over the testing history of 10/10 BKK riders and
  rises for 10/12 CNX riders. The performance decline is front-loaded. With rider + day FE within
  region: BKK +3.0 (p=.50), CNX +1.2 (p=.31).
- Decline anatomy: median RT 408->476 ms, lapses 21->34%, fastest-10% RT only +22 ms, false starts
  flat. That is tail-driven (vigilance/effort), not an instrument shift.
- Within-day design (within_day.py): only 4% of the plan's exposure variance is within rider-day.
  Change between consecutive same-day tests vs air between them: -1.2 [-4.9, 2.6]; with region x
  day FE -5.8 [-14.3, 2.8]. The placebo (air in 4h after the 2nd test) is significant: -5.0,
  p=.032; with FE -9.5, p=.013. It is not explained by riding time. Mechanism unknown.
- Drift removal (remove_drift.py): per-rider smooth trends or rider-week FE remove the 0.28 Hz
  decline but keep only 69% / 10-14% of PM2.5 variance. No variant is significant. With rider-week
  FE the pre-test estimate (-4.3, -4.0) exceeds the placebo (-1.8, -1.4), p ~ 0.09: a hypothesis,
  not a finding.
- Adjacent study days (adjacent_days.py): consecutive days (median 2 d apart, |dPM| median 13
  ug/m3) give -1.4 [-6.1, +3.3]; symmetric neighbour comparison -2.3 [-12.6, +8.0]. Ambient
  temperature hints at slowing (2.5-3.6 ms per C) but p = 0.07-0.15 with rider x day clustering.
- Bounds (bounds_and_variance.py): per +50 ug/m3, worst case +3.4 ms (same city, same day,
  declared), +4.3 ms (plan of record, declared), +5.6 to +13.5 ms (post-hoc designs).
